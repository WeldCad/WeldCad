WeldCAD Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Short version: WeldCAD does not collect, sell or share any of your information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your designs stay on your device unless you choose to send them somewhere.

What WeldCAD stores, and where

  • Your designs, settings, prices, saved views and version history are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • Imported 3D models and traced photos are stored only on your device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server. WeldCAD works fully offline.

Files you share

When you use Save, Export, Print or Share, WeldCAD hands the file to your device's system share sheet, Files app, printer or save dialog. Where it goes from there (iCloud Drive, email, AirDrop, a printer) is up to you, and is handled by Apple or the service you pick, under their privacy policies.

Camera and photos

WeldCAD asks for the camera or your photo library only when you choose a photo to trace in the 2D view. The photo is used on your device to draw over and is never uploaded.

Augmented reality

The AR view uses Apple's built-in AR Quick Look. WeldCAD creates the 3D model on your device and Apple's system viewer shows it with your camera. WeldCAD does not receive or store camera images.

Purchases (WeldCAD Pro)

WeldCAD Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment, your Apple Account and your subscription; WeldCAD never sees your name, email, card or billing details. The app only asks Apple whether this device's Apple Account has an active WeldCAD Pro purchase, so it can turn Pro features on. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To count the free cut-file downloads, WeldCAD stores a single number in your device's keychain. It contains nothing about you, is never sent to the developer or any server, and is used only to know how many free downloads are left.

Children

WeldCAD does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset app in WeldCAD, or delete the app, to remove everything it stored on your device. Files you exported elsewhere are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact button on the WeldCAD support page.
