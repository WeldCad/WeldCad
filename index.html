Help

What's free, and what is WeldCAD Pro?

Designing is free: 3D design, templates, sheet metal, cut list, cost, the 2D drawing view, the plasma designer and the table simulator. WeldCAD Pro adds unlimited G-code, DXF and SVG cut files (your first 3 are free), picture tracing, auto-nesting, STL and CSV export, DXF drawings and printing.

I bought Pro but it isn't turned on

Open WeldCAD → Settings → See WeldCAD Pro → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro also works on your other iPhone, iPad and Mac with that account, and with Family Sharing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WeldCAD Pro. On Mac: App Store → your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which plasma tables does the G-code work with?

WeldCAD has post-processors for Langmuir FireControl, Mach3/Mach4, Torchmate, UCCNC, LinuxCNC/QtPlasmaC, MASSO, GRBL, ArcDroid and generic G-code. You can also export DXF or SVG for your own CAM software. Try a test cut on scrap first.

Where are my designs saved? Do I need an account?

No account. Everything is saved on your device and the app works offline. Use Save or Share to back up a design to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or email.
